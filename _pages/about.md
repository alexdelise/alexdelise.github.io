Hi there! My name is Alex and I am a junior at Florida State University pursuing two degrees in Applied and Computational Mathematics and Computational Science. I am passionate about developing data-driven machine learning models that are both robust and interpretable, and about advancing our mathematical understanding of why they succeed or fail.

In Spring 2026 I will begin my honors thesis in generative compressed sensing under my advisor (Nick Dexter)[https://www.sc.fsu.edu/people?uid=nd22f] in the Department of Scientific Computing. 

In my previous summers, I have done two NSF Research Experience for Undergradutes. In Summer 2025, I was at Emory University at the (Computational Mathematics for Data Science REU)[https://www.math.emory.edu/site/cmds-reuret/] under the supervision of (Matthias Chung)[https://sites.google.com/view/matthias-chung/home]. In Summer 2024, I was at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville at the (Quantum Algorithims and Optimization REU)[https://tickle.utk.edu/ise/research/research-experience-for-undergraduates/] under the supervision of (James Ostrowski)[https://tickle.utk.edu/ise/faculty/james-ostrowski/].
